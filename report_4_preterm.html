Preterm & LBW babies — the four life-saving interventions

Of all newborns, those born preterm or with low birth weight have the highest risk and the largest gap in care quality. Four interventions — antenatal corticosteroids, magnesium sulfate, kangaroo mother care, continuous KMC — can change a life trajectory in the first hours.

Kangaroo Mother Care (KMC) for preterm and low-birthweight babies

Continuous skin-to-skin contact, frequent breastfeeding, and close monitoring — KMC reduces preterm sepsis, hypothermia, and mortality. WHO recommends KMC alongside (not as substitute for) standard care, including continuous KMC ≥20 hours/day for the highest-impact dose.

! Magnesium sulfate is the largest knowledge-practice gap — available at 65% of facilities but reaching only 37% of eligible <32-week deliveries.
