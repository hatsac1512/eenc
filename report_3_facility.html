In focus

Facility readiness underpins safe newborn care

Adequate handwashing, alcohol-based hand rub, clean resuscitation areas, and reliable supply of life-saving medicines (oxytocin, antenatal corticosteroids, magnesium sulfate) determine whether EENC can be delivered consistently.

Figure 10

Hand-washing resources by room type

Five infrastructure criteria across 528 rooms in 207 facilities. The composite "all four criteria met" (clean sink + running water + soap + single-use towels) is the binding constraint.

! Postnatal rooms are the IPC weak spot: 75% have clean sinks but only 27% meet all four criteria. The single-use towel/dryer is the main missing element across all room types. Operating rooms and NCUs perform best (53% and 48% all-criteria).
