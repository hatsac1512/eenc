Key findings · at a glance

EENC progress in the Western Pacific

Tracked against the Action Plan for Healthy Newborn Infants in the Western Pacific (2014–2020, extended to 2030) — three layers of results: outputs (system reach), outcomes (care practices), and impact (mortality).

91%
Outputs · Facilities implementing EENC
6,446 health facilities have introduced EENC — 91% of facilities providing childbirth services across eight priority countries (excluding China). 42,403 health facility staff have been coached on EENC. Action Plan target: ≥80%.
50–92%
Outcomes · Care-practice coverage
50% of newborns receive immediate skin-to-skin contact · 92% exclusive breastfeeding · 70% of babies ≤2000 g receive kangaroo mother care · 56% of births have a completed partograph.
−12 to −56%
Impact · Decline in neonatal mortality
12–56% decrease in neonatal mortality across 6 of 7 priority countries. Aligned with the Action Plan target of NMR ≤10 per 1,000 live births and ≥90% births in sub-national areas attended by skilled birth attendants.

About this report

The 2023 regional EENC review is the third measurement round (after 2017 and 2019) coordinated by the WHO Regional Office for the Western Pacific. Data are collected through a standardised facility assessment combining record review, mother interviews, observed deliveries, and infrastructure inspection.

All values shown reflect the published 2023 regional report. Where indicator definitions evolved between rounds (notably the SSC composite indicator), trends use harmonised definitions.

Methods

  • 1,581 term mothers (Checklist 1.1)
  • 227 preterm/LBW (Checklist 1.2)
  • 1,117 chart reviews (2.1, 2.2)
  • 531 observed births (3.1, 3.2)
  • 208 facility commodity audits (3.3, 4)
