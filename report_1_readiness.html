Building blocks of EENC programme readiness

Strong national programmes start with policies, action plans, and integration into pre-service curricula. The 10 readiness benchmarks track each country's institutional commitment to scaling EENC.

10 EENC scale-up readiness benchmarks — status by country, 2023

National-level programme infrastructure across seven priority countries. Achieved = benchmark fully met; Partial = some criteria met; Not achieved = criteria not met. Data validated for 98.2% of benchmarks reported.

Figure 2

Regional benchmark trajectory 2017 → 2023

Six benchmarks have improved across the region; two are flat at 100%; two have regressed — funded implementation planning and stakeholder coordination. Trend computed across all priority countries reporting in each round.

! Funded 12-month plan dropped 78% → 43% between 2019 and 2023. Strong technical scaffolding (curricula, guides) is in place — but domestic financing and coordination architecture have weakened, putting clinical gains at risk if external funding contracts.
Figure 3

Proportion of health facilities providing EENC, by country

% of childbirth facilities that have introduced EENC in 2017, 2019/2020, and 2022/2023. WPRO target: 80%. China reports a denominator covering all 25,860 facilities nation-wide.

! Vietnam reached 100% in 2023 (from 26% in 2017) and PNG passed 80% — both transformation stories. Lao PDR regressed from 64% (2019) to 43% (2023), the only outright decline. China's 1.8% reflects the size of the national denominator, not lack of progress (9,069 facilities now implementing).
